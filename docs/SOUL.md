# SOUL.md

## Emotional North Star

This site should feel like discovering a hidden character-select screen drifting in space, made by someone who genuinely loves building things.

## Personality Balance

60% professional competence
40% personal charm

## What It Must Communicate

* Craftsmanship
* Curiosity
* Playfulness with restraint
* Technical confidence
* Care for detail

## What It Must Avoid

* Immature energy
* Overdesigned chaos
* Loud colors everywhere
* Slow or janky interactions
* Empty gimmicks

## User Reaction Targets

* Wow
* Creative
* Knowledgeable

## Writing Tone

Clear, concise, intelligent. Mostly professional copy. Let the interface carry the fun.

## Experience Notes

* Every click should feel rewarding.
* Animation should support clarity, not distract.
* Humor should be optional and charming.
* The portfolio should still work if users ignore the gimmick and scan content quickly.
* Motion should feel weightless, calm, and polished.

## Build Philosophy

Ship a strong v1 with placeholder art, then iterate assets and polish.
